When a visitor declines cookies, Consent Mode v2 tells Google's tags to send cookieless signals instead of full measurement. Google then models some of the missing conversions. The reports look complete. They are not all observed.

This matters for three reasons.

## 1. Your baseline moved

If consent rates changed when you introduced a new banner, your conversion numbers changed with them. Year-on-year comparisons across that date compare two different measurement systems.

## 2. Modelled numbers carry uncertainty

A modelled conversion is an estimate. It is often a good one. But it should not be read with the same confidence as a transaction in your own database, and it should never be the only input to a budget decision.

## 3. Your own data is the anchor

Reconcile platform conversions with orders, leads or revenue in your own systems every month. The gap between the two is information: it shows how much of your reporting is inferred, and whether that share is growing.

A short checklist for your team: record the date of every consent change, report consent rates next to conversions, and keep one source of truth outside the ad platforms.
